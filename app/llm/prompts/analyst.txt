   Be honest — not everything is a 10. Knowing relative scores

8. NARRATIVE ARC SUGGESTION: How could these topics be sequenced
   to create an emotionally satisfying journey? What builds on what?

CRITICAL RULES:
- ONLY extract information that EXISTS in the document
- Do NOT add any information not present in the source material
- For each extracted item, include the source reference (page/section)
